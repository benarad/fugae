The analysis is worked out by a program and is a first reading, not an authority.

Keys: space plays and pauses; ← and → go to the previous and the next section, and with shift to the previous and the next entry; L repeats the section.

Notation is drawn by Verovio. The piano is the Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (a Yamaha C5, retuned by Markus Fiedler; CC BY 3.0), cut down for the web. The harpsichords are from the Versilian Community Sample Library (public domain), cut down likewise, but for the General MIDI one, which is from the FluidR3 GM sound set. The synthesizer is made by the page.
