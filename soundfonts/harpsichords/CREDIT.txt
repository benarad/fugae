These harpsichord notes are cut down from the Versilian Community Sample Library (Versilian Studios and
contributors), which is in the public domain (Creative Commons 0): https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
Changes: each note cut to start at its pluck and to last at most 10 seconds, the sound of each key let
go beside it, and both encoded as AAC. index.json in each folder gives each note's measured pitch.
